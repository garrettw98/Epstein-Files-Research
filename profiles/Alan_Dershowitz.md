# ⚖️ Alan Dershowitz (Harvard Law Professor)

> **Status**: **ATTORNEY / ACCUSED (Recanted)** (Evidence Level: ◐ DOCUMENTED)

## The Connection
Alan Dershowitz was one of Jeffrey Epstein's star defense attorneys. He helped negotiate the infamous 2008 Plea Deal (Non-Prosecution Agreement).

## The Accusations
*   **Virginia Giuffre**: For years, Giuffre alleged that Epstein trafficked her to Dershowitz for sex on multiple occasions.
*   **The Defamation War**: Dershowitz vehemently denied this, calling Giuffre a liar. They sued each other for defamation in a bitter, years-long legal battle.

## The Resolution (2022)
In November 2022, the lawsuits were dropped.
*   **The Statement**: Virginia Giuffre released a statement saying: *"I may have made a mistake in identifying Mr. Dershowitz."*
*   **The Implication**: She admitted she might have confused him with someone else, effectively clearing his name regarding the sexual assault.

## The Flight Logs
Dershowitz admits to flying on Epstein's plane frequently, but maintains it was for legal business or family travel to his own home in Martha's Vineyard.

## Verdict
**Defense Attorney.** While he was a key architect of the legal deal that protected Epstein, the sexual assault allegations against him personally have been withdrawn by the accuser. In 2026 he publicly volunteered to testify to Congress, then withdrew from a scheduled interview citing an unspecified "legal barrier."

## 2026 Update: The House Oversight Testimony Standoff

- **Jun 10, 2026**: After the committee's interview of Epstein assistant Lesley Groff and a meeting with survivors, Chairman James Comer said he would ask Dershowitz (and acting AG Todd Blanche) to come in. Dershowitz said he wanted his testimony "videotaped, under oath and open to the public," adding: "I'm prepared to testify about everything... I will personally not invoke privilege. I have nothing to hide." [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/5918269-comer-dershowitz-blanche-interviews-epstein/), [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/5918579-dershowitz-epstein-house-committee/)
- **Jun 12, 2026**: Comer formally requested a transcribed interview; it was set for **Jul 20**. [Comer letter (PDF)](https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Dershowitz-Letter-061226.pdf), [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/5934847-dershowitz-house-epstein-interview/)
- **Jul 15, 2026**: His counsel told the committee he "cannot now testify in Congress concerning the matters in which the Committee are interested." He told the Harvard Crimson his attorneys had identified a legal barrier; he declined to say what it was and said he remained "anxious" to testify. [Harvard Crimson](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2026/7/18/dershowitz-epstein-testimony/), [NewsNation](https://www.newsnationnow.com/politics/dershowitz-anxious-testify-epstein-abrupt-house-cancellation/)
- **Jul 20-21, 2026**: He did not appear. Comer called it "unacceptable," requested a videotaped interview on **Sep 2, 2026**, said the committee "will not tolerate any further delay," and warned it would consider compulsory process. [ABC](https://abcnews.com/US/comer-calls-unacceptable-after-alan-dershowitz-fails-panel/story?id=134952381), [Comer letter (PDF)](https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Dershowitz-Letter-FINAL-7.21.26.pdf), [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/21/james-comer-demands-alan-dershowitz-testify-jeffrey-epstein/)
- **Status (Sep 24, 2026)**: No public report was found confirming whether the Sep 2 interview occurred or whether a subpoena was issued. See [House Oversight Witness Program (2026)](../topics/The_House_Oversight_Witness_Program_2026.md).
