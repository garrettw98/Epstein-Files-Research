# Andrew Farkas (Real Estate Investor; Hasty Pudding Institute Chair)

> **Status**: **DOCUMENTED BUSINESS AND PERSONAL TIES — NOT ACCUSED OF A CRIME**; says he never "conducted myself inappropriately"; faced resignation calls at Harvard's Hasty Pudding (Feb 2026); no resignation reported as of Sep 24, 2026
> **Last updated**: Sep 24, 2026

## Who He Is

Andrew L. Farkas, a 1982 Harvard graduate, is a New York real estate and banking investor (Island Capital Group) and chair of the Hasty Pudding Institute, the alumni body behind Harvard's Hasty Pudding Theatricals.

## What the 2026 Release Showed

- **Correspondence**: Reporting on the Jan 30 release counted nearly 2,000 emails between Farkas and Epstein over more than a decade. In them Farkas called Epstein "one of the blessings" in his life and "one of the bravest men I've ever known." [Boing Boing (summarizing NYT)](https://boingboing.net/2026/02/09/billionaire-called-epstein-one-of-the-blessings-in-my-life-in-newly-released-emails.html), [GV Wire](https://gvwire.com/2026/02/09/billionaire-called-epstein-a-blessing-as-they-made-deals/)
- **The marina deal**: The New York Times reported that Farkas partnered with Epstein in a U.S. Virgin Islands marina deal that helped Epstein save more than $300 million in taxes. This is the Times's characterization; no tax violation by Farkas has been alleged by any authority.
- **Alternate executor**: DOJ records show Farkas was named as an alternate executor in Epstein's 2012 will.
- **Hasty Pudding donations**: Farkas repeatedly asked Epstein to give to Hasty Pudding. Epstein gave about $50,000 a year from roughly 2013 to 2019, more than $300,000 in all (the Harvard Crimson reported at least $375,000 to the Institute). [Harvard Crimson (Nov 2025)](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2025/11/28/pudding-epstein-linked-donations/)

[Harvard Crimson](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2026/2/21/hpt-graduate-advisory-board-resignation/)

- **Status (Sep 24)**: No resignation or Harvard finding had been reported. Harvard's review remains open. See [The Academic Fallout (2026)](../topics/The_Academic_Fallout_2026.md#harvard-the-review-and-its-critics).
