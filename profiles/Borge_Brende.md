# Børge Brende

> **Status**: **RESIGNED** — former World Economic Forum president and CEO (resigned Feb 26, 2026); not accused of any crime
> **Role in Repo**: Former Norwegian foreign minister (2013-2017) whose Epstein contacts ended his WEF tenure
> **Last updated**: Sep 23, 2026

## Summary

Børge Brende, Norway's foreign minister from 2013 to 2017, led the World Economic Forum for more than eight years. The January 2026 DOJ release showed he arranged dinners with Epstein at Epstein's New York home in 2018 and 2019, the second planned weeks before Epstein's arrest. Brende said he met Epstein only in business settings and was unaware of his criminal background. After a WEF review, he resigned on February 26, 2026. Reporting noted no indication that Brende engaged in wrongdoing.
