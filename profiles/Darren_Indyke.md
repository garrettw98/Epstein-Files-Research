# Darren Indyke

> **Status**: **REPORTED SUBJECT OF FEDERAL CRIMINAL INVESTIGATION** — not charged; denies wrongdoing (Evidence Level: ◐ MAJOR REPORTING)
> **Role in Repo**: Epstein's longtime lawyer; co-executor of the Estate of Jeffrey E. Epstein
> **Last updated**: Sep 23, 2026

## Summary

Darren K. Indyke was Jeffrey Epstein's personal lawyer for more than a decade and, with accountant Richard Kahn, is co-executor of his estate. Both witnessed the "1953 Trust" Epstein signed on August 8, 2019, two days before his death. On September 23, 2026, the Wall Street Journal reported that federal prosecutors in Manhattan have opened a criminal investigation into Indyke and Kahn. They deny wrongdoing, and no charges have been filed.
