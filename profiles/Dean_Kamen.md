# 🔧 Dean Kamen (Inventor / FIRST Robotics Founder)

## The Connection
Dean Kamen — inventor of the Segway, founder of the youth robotics organization **FIRST**, and leader of the government-funded **ARMI** research project — had a documented multi-year relationship with Epstein that included visits to Florida, the Caribbean, and correspondence with mutual acquaintances.

## Key Evidence (2026 Files)

### Island Visit Confirmed
Documents released January 30, 2026 include an email from Kamen thanking Epstein for **"an incredible visit"** to Little St. James, confirming he spent time on Epstein's private island.

### Relationship Timeline
- Correspondence spanning **several years**
- Trips to **Florida and the Caribbean**
- Scuttled plans for Kamen to visit Epstein's **ranch in New Mexico**
- A photo of Kamen with Epstein and **Richard Branson** was released by Democrats on the House Oversight Committee on **December 12, 2025**

## Organizational Fallout

### FIRST
On **February 1, 2026**, the board of **FIRST** (the Manchester, NH-based youth science organization Kamen founded) placed him on **immediate leave from "all activities"** while it reviews the revelations about his Epstein relationship.

### ARMI
The board of directors for **ARMI**, the government-funded Manchester research project led by Kamen, is also meeting to discuss next steps.

## Kamen's Statement

Kamen stated:
- He has **"no knowledge of any of the horrific crimes of Jeffrey Epstein"** other than from news reports
- Epstein reached out regarding **clean water and distributed power projects** in developing countries
- After initial meetings, Kamen says Epstein's only interest was **"self-promotion"**
- He avoided further meetings
- He **"of course regret[s] even those limited interactions"**

## Verdict
**Documented Island Visitor.** Kamen visited Little St. James and maintained a multi-year relationship with Epstein. He resigned from the FIRST board in March 2026 after an independent board review found no evidence of misconduct; he remains with ARMI and Sequel Med Tech after separate reviews. No allegations of wrongdoing beyond the association itself.
