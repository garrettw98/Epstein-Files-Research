# Doug Band

> **Status**: **HOUSE OVERSIGHT WITNESS** (not accused of any crime) (Evidence Level: ● TRANSCRIPT)
> **Role in Repo**: Longtime aide and counselor to Bill Clinton; questioned about Clinton-Epstein interactions
> **Last updated**: Sep 23, 2026

## Summary

Doug Band was Bill Clinton's longtime personal aide and a co-founder of the consulting firm Teneo. He was on House Oversight Chairman James Comer's March 3, 2026 list of seven people asked for transcribed interviews. On June 30, 2026 he testified voluntarily for about four hours.

## Testimony (June 30, 2026)

- Described his personal contact with Epstein as **limited, infrequent, and largely incidental** to his work for Clinton.
- Said he barely knew Epstein, did not recall receiving gifts from him, and had no knowledge of Epstein's abuse or other crimes.
- **Repeatedly said he could not recall** his involvement on several Epstein-related topics.

Sources: [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/doug-band-former-aide-bill-clinton-questioned-clintons/story?id=134313410), [MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/news/former-bill-clinton-aide-testifying-before-house-oversight-on-epstein-ties), [Oversight transcript release](https://oversight.house.gov/release/oversight-committee-releases-leon-black-and-doug-band-transcripts/)
