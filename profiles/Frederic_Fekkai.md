# Frédéric Fekkai (Celebrity Hairstylist)

> **Status**: **ACCUSED IN TESTIMONY AND CIVIL SUIT — DENIES; NOT CHARGED**
> **Last updated**: Sep 24, 2026

## Who He Is

Frédéric Fekkai is a French-born hairstylist and founder of a salon and hair-care brand. His name appears in Epstein-related correspondence, and in 2026 he was accused in congressional testimony and in a civil lawsuit. **All of the claims below are allegations. Fekkai denies them and has not been charged with any crime.**

## 2026 Timeline

- **May 21, 2026**: Former Epstein assistant **Sarah Kellen** told House Oversight in a transcribed interview that Fekkai sexually assaulted her. His representative denied it. See [Sarah Kellen](Sarah_Kellen.md).
- **Jun 4, 2026**: Chairman James Comer and other Republicans asked DOJ to investigate Kellen's allegations against Fekkai and former Miami Beach mayor Philip Levine. No DOJ action has been reported. [House Oversight](https://oversight.house.gov/release/chairman-comer-and-republican-lawmakers-seek-doj-investigation-into-sexual-assault-allegations-made-during-epstein-investigation/)
- **Aug 31, 2026**: Rep. Thomas Massie named him on the House floor among 14 people he said should be investigated. See [The Massie Floor Speech](../topics/The_Massie_Floor_Speech_Aug_2026.md).
- **Sep 1, 2026**: Seven women sued him in New York Supreme Court, alleging his salon groomed young women for Epstein, including bleaching their hair blond at Epstein's direction, and served as a "perks machine" for Epstein for more than 15 years. The case was brought under New York City's reopened Gender-Motivated Violence Act window. [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/04/politics/frederic-fekkai-epstein-files-lawsuit), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/alisondurkee/2026/09/02/epstein-victims-sue-top-hairdresser-after-rep-massie-names-alleged-co-conspirators/)

## His Response

His representative said Fekkai had no knowledge of Epstein's trafficking or criminal conduct and could not have known whether any victims were among the many clients his salons served. He also denied Kellen's allegation.
