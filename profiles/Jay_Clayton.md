# Jay Clayton (Former SDNY U.S. Attorney; Director of National Intelligence)

> **Status**: **GOVERNMENT OFFICIAL** — Director of National Intelligence since Aug 3, 2026; U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of New York 2025-Jul 2026
> **Last updated**: Sep 24, 2026

## Why He Matters

As U.S. attorney in Manhattan, Clayton oversaw the office that prosecuted Maxwell and holds much of the federal Epstein record. In November 2025 Attorney General Pam Bondi put him in charge of the Epstein investigation President Trump requested into Democrats and JPMorgan, and reporting said he was also tasked with overseeing redaction of victims' personal information in the DOJ release. In 2026 he became Director of National Intelligence, the post that oversees intelligence on Epstein's foreign contacts.

## Timeline

- **Nov 14, 2025**: Trump asked DOJ to investigate Epstein's relationships with Bill Clinton, Larry Summers, Reid Hoffman, JPMorgan Chase, "and many other people and institutions." Bondi named Clayton to lead it the same day. Trump named only Democrats. The FBI and DOJ had said in July 2025 that their review found no evidence to predicate an investigation of uncharged third parties. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/14/trump-doj-jeffrey-epstein-clinton-jpmorgan-summers.html), [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/trump-epstein-bondi-clinton-larry-summers-reid-hoffman-jpmorgan-chase-rcna243971), [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/11/15/politics/jay-clayton-sdny-epstein-investigation)
- **Jan 30, 2026**: DOJ's release exposed some survivors' identifying information; senators later questioned him about it. See [The Redaction and Context Scandal](../topics/The_Redaction_and_Context_Scandal.md).
- **May 22 - Jun 11, 2026**: Tulsi Gabbard announced her resignation as DNI (effective Jun 30), and Trump nominated Clayton to replace her on Jun 11. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/29/nx-s1-5910749/senate-confirms-jay-clayton-as-director-of-national-intelligence)
- **Jul 15, 2026**: At his Senate Intelligence Committee hearing, Sen. Martin Heinrich (D-N.M.) pressed him on DOJ's refusal to give New Mexico unredacted Zorro Ranch evidence, on DOJ's July 23, 2019 request that New Mexico "stand down" from its own investigation, and on the release of victims' email addresses and nude photos. New Mexico's attorney general disputed Clayton's account of ongoing dialogue, saying the only federal contact was a call saying DOJ would not cooperate. [Heinrich](https://www.heinrich.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/heinrich-presses-jay-clayton-on-doj-obstruction-of-new-mexico-investigation-into-jeffrey-epsteins-crimes-at-zorro-ranch), [NM Political Report](https://nmpoliticalreport.com/2026/07/15/video-heinrich-presses-trumps-spy-pick-on-epstein-cover-up-victim-data-leak/)
- **Jul 20, 2026**: Addressed, as DNI nominee, in Rep. Jamie Raskin's letter asking whether Epstein acted as an unregistered foreign agent. See [FARA Inquiry](../topics/The_FARA_Foreign_Agent_Inquiry.md).
- **Jul 29, 2026**: Confirmed by the Senate. James "Jamie" McDonald, appointed by Chief Judge Laura Taylor Swain, was sworn in as SDNY U.S. attorney the same day. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/29/nx-s1-5910749/senate-confirms-jay-clayton-as-director-of-national-intelligence), [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/jamie-mcdonald-assumes-role-united-states-attorney-southern-district-new-york)
- **Aug 3, 2026**: Sworn in as the ninth Director of National Intelligence. [ODNI](https://www.odni.gov/14064/pr-12-26/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/jay-clayton-sworn-in-as-us-director-of-national-intelligence.html)
- **Sep 23, 2026**: The Wall Street Journal reported that SDNY, now under McDonald, is investigating estate executors Darren Indyke and Richard Kahn. See [The Estate Executors Investigation](../topics/The_Estate_Executors_Investigation.md).

## What Is Unresolved

- The Trump-requested investigation of Clinton, Summers, Hoffman, and JPMorgan has produced no public charges or report. Whether it continues under McDonald is unknown.
- Whether the executor probe grew out of the investigation Clayton was assigned is not public.
