# 🏦 Jes Staley (Former Barclays CEO)

> **Role**: **The Banker / Financial Enabler**
> **Status**: **BANNED FROM BANKING**

## The Connection
Jes Staley was a senior executive at **JPMorgan Chase** (head of the Private Bank) and later CEO of **Barclays**. He was Epstein's primary contact at the bank.

## The Evidence (The "Snow White" Emails)
*   **1,200 Emails**: Staley and Epstein exchanged over 1,000 emails between 2008 and 2012.
*   **The Code**: They used cryptic phrases like "Snow White" and shared photos of young women.
*   **The Visits**: Staley visited Epstein's private island (Little St. James) and was photographed on the "Lolita Express."

## The "Protection"
*   **Overriding Compliance**: When JPMorgan's internal safety systems flagged Epstein's wire transfers as "human trafficking," Staley allegedly intervened to keep Epstein as a client, arguing he brought in too much revenue.
*   **Post-Conviction Loyalty**: Staley maintained the relationship even after Epstein's 2008 conviction, visiting him in jail and at his office.

## The Fall
*   **2021**: Resigned as CEO of Barclays after UK regulators shared preliminary findings of their investigation.
*   **2023**: The UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) **banned** him from the banking industry and fined him **£1.8 million**, stating he "recklessly misled" them about his relationship with Epstein.
