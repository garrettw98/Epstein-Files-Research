# Katie Phang

> **Status**: **LITIGANT / JOURNALIST** (not accused of wrongdoing)
> **Role in Repo**: Plaintiff in *Phang v. Blanche*, the first lawsuit to produce court orders enforcing the Epstein Files Transparency Act
> **Last updated**: Sep 23, 2026

## Summary

Katie Phang is an attorney, former cable-news host, and independent journalist. On April 27, 2026 she sued the Justice Department in federal court in Washington, alleging DOJ was violating the Epstein Files Transparency Act by withholding records and redacting beyond what the law allows. Judge Emmet Sullivan granted her a preliminary injunction on June 25, 2026, and the case has since produced in camera review of disputed records, contempt warnings, and a Sept. 24, 2026 compliance deadline.
