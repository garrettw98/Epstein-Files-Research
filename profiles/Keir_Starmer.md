# Keir Starmer

> **Status**: **FORMER PRIME MINISTER** — resigned as UK Labour leader and prime minister (announced Jun 22, 2026); not accused of any crime
> **Role in Repo**: Appointed Peter Mandelson as ambassador to Washington; the fallout ended his premiership
> **Last updated**: Sep 23, 2026

## Summary

Keir Starmer appointed Peter Mandelson as UK ambassador to the United States in early 2025 and dismissed him in September 2025 after Mandelson's Epstein ties surfaced. In 2026, government files showed Starmer had been warned about the reputational risk, and reporting revealed Mandelson had failed security vetting before Foreign Office officials overrode the recommendation. Opposition leaders accused Starmer of misleading Parliament. After a Cabinet resignation and months of pressure, he announced his resignation on June 22, 2026 and left Parliament on September 1.
