# Maurene Comey (Former SDNY Prosecutor)

> **Status**: **FIRED JULY 2025; WRONGFUL-TERMINATION SUIT PROCEEDING** (not accused of wrongdoing)
> **Last updated**: Sep 24, 2026

## Who She Is

Maurene Comey spent nearly a decade as an assistant U.S. attorney in the Southern District of New York. She worked on the 2019 prosecution of Jeffrey Epstein and the 2021 trial of Ghislaine Maxwell, and later on the Sean Combs case. She is the daughter of former FBI Director James Comey.

## Timeline

- **Jul 16, 2025**: DOJ fired her without a stated cause, during the backlash over DOJ's July 2025 statement that it would release no more Epstein files. [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/justice-department/maurene-comey-daughter-james-comey-fired-sean-combs-ghislaine-maxwell-rcna219236), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/07/16/maurene-comey-prosecutor-epstein-fired-trump-administration)
- **Sep 2025**: She sued, alleging the only plausible explanation for her firing was President Trump's animus toward her father or her perceived politics. DOJ moved to dismiss in December, arguing the case belonged before an administrative board. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/12/3/us-justice-department-seeks-to-dismiss-lawsuit-over-maurene-comeys-firing)
- **Apr 28, 2026**: U.S. District Judge Jesse Furman ruled her suit can proceed in federal court. He noted that the only reason DOJ gave for the firing was Article II of the Constitution, and called her "by all accounts, an exemplary Assistant United States Attorney." The ruling addressed jurisdiction, not the merits. An initial pretrial conference was set for May 28. [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/judge-maurene-comey-firing-justice-department/), [AP via US News](https://www.usnews.com/news/best-states/new-york/articles/2026-04-28/judge-rules-that-fired-prosecutor-maurene-comeys-lawsuit-belongs-in-federal-court)

## Why It Matters Here

She is one of the few career prosecutors with direct knowledge of the federal Epstein and Maxwell cases, and her firing is part of the record critics cite when questioning DOJ's handling of those cases. No court has found why she was fired.
