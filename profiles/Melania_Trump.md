# Melania Trump (First Lady)

> **Status**: **NOT ACCUSED OF ANY CRIME; DENIES ANY EPSTEIN TIES**; pursuing sanctions against author Michael Wolff (motion pending as of Sep 24, 2026)
> **Last updated**: Sep 24, 2026

## Why She Appears Here

Melania Trump appears in this database because of claims about how she met Donald Trump and how close she was to Epstein's social circle, and because of the lawsuits and legal threats she has used to answer those claims. No survivor has accused her of wrongdoing, and she has not been accused of a crime. Photos from the late 1990s and early 2000s show the Trumps at social events that Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell also attended. What those photos show about the relationship is disputed, and she denies any friendship with Epstein.

## Timeline

- **August 2025**: Her lawyer Alejandro Brito threatened Hunter Biden with a defamation suit seeking more than $1 billion after Biden repeated, in interviews, a claim attributed to Michael Wolff that Epstein introduced her to Donald Trump. She denies that claim. [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/11336130/melania-trump-hunter-biden-lawsuit/)
- **October 2025**: Her lawyers threatened Wolff with a $1 billion suit over his statements about her and Epstein. Wolff then sued her first in New York state court under the state's anti-SLAPP law, asking for a declaration that his statements were protected. The case was moved to federal court. [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/media/author-michael-wolff-sues-melania-trump-over-legal-threat-against-his-epstein-connection-claims), [Kirkus](https://www.kirkusreviews.com/news-and-features/articles/author-michael-wolff-sues-melania-trump/)
- **Apr 9, 2026**: In remarks at the White House she said the "lies linking me with the disgraceful Jeffrey Epstein need to end today," denied any ties to him, and called on Congress to give survivors a public hearing. House Oversight Chairman James Comer said the next day that survivors would get one. As of Sep 24 no majority-led public survivor hearing had been located. Survivors' reactions were mixed. [ABC](https://abcnews.com/Politics/melania-trump-declares-epsteins-victim/story?id=131891150), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/10/epstein-melania-trump-victims-house-hearings.html)
- **May 22, 2026**: U.S. District Judge Mary Kay Vyskocil (S.D.N.Y.) dismissed Wolff's suit in a 45-page ruling. She called it "textbook bad-faith forum shopping" and wrote: "Plaintiff asks for a declaration that, if the first lady sues him, he deserves to win. That is not how the federal courts work." The ruling did not decide whether Wolff's statements are true. [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/melania-trump-wins-dismissal-of-michael-wolffs-anti-slapp-suit/), [Volokh Conspiracy](https://reason.com/volokh/2026/05/23/judge-dismisses-author-michael-wolffs-lawsuit-over-melania-trumps-defamation-litigation-threat/)
- **Jul 1, 2026**: At a conference, Vyskocil cautioned against a sanctions motion, saying, "I wonder if it's in the interest of the parties to continue litigating in this court." [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/judge-cautions-melania-trump-against-trying-to-sanction-journalist-michael-wolff/)
- **Jul 21, 2026**: She moved for sanctions anyway, seeking her costs and attorneys' fees. Her lawyers called Wolff's suit "a fundraising drive and a political spectacle" and argued he had manufactured a jurisdictional dispute. Wolff's opposition was due Aug 17 and her reply Aug 28. **No ruling located as of Sep 24.** She has not filed the threatened $1 billion defamation suit against Wolff. [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/melania-trump-files-for-sanctions-against-journalist-michael-wolff/), [Political Wire](https://politicalwire.com/2026/07/23/melania-trump-seeks-sanctions-against-michael-wolff/), [TMZ](https://www.tmz.com/2026/07/21/melania-trump-wants-court-sanction-michael-wolff/)

## Evidence Posture

- **Documented**: the legal threats, the Wolff suit and its dismissal, the sanctions motion, and her Apr 9 denial.
- **Disputed**: Wolff's and others' claims about how she met Donald Trump and her place in Epstein's social circle. She denies them, and no court has ruled on whether they are true.
- **Not alleged**: any crime or any abuse of a survivor.
