# Mona Juul (Norwegian Diplomat)

> **Status**: **CHARGED (AGGRAVATED CORRUPTION, FEB 9, 2026); NOT TRIED; RESIGNED AS AMBASSADOR (FEB 8, 2026)**

## Summary

Mona Juul is a senior Norwegian diplomat who, with her husband **Terje Rød-Larsen**, was part of the back-channel team behind the 1993 Oslo Accords. At the time of the January 2026 release she was Norway's ambassador to Jordan and Iraq. The files documented financial and personal ties between the couple and Jeffrey Epstein, including a reported **$10 million** bequest to their two children in a version of Epstein's will.

## Timeline

- **Early Feb 2026**: Norway's Ministry of Foreign Affairs suspended her as ambassador pending review (the repo's international timeline records this on Feb 5).
- **Feb 8, 2026**: She **stepped down as ambassador**. [Government of Norway](https://www.regjeringen.no/en/whats-new/mona-juul-steps-down-as-ambassador/id3148469), [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/norwegian-ambassador-resigns-amid-scrutiny-over-her-contacts-with-epstein)
- **Feb 9, 2026**: Økokrim **charged Juul** with aggravated corruption and her husband with **complicity** in aggravated corruption. Investigators are examining a loan from Epstein, his alleged role in the couple's below-market purchase of an Oslo flat in 2018, a 2011 island trip, and payment for home help. [News in English](https://www.newsinenglish.no/2026/02/08/top-diplomats-dramatic-decline/), [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/son-of-epstein-associate-under-investigation-dies-by-suicide-11899731)
- **Apr 27-28, 2026**: Økokrim and French investigators formed a **joint investigation team** covering the couple's case and a related French case. Senior state prosecutor Marianne Bender said the arrangement means neither country has to send a new letter of request each time it needs information. [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/04/27/norway-and-france-launch-joint-police-probe-into-diplomats-alleged-links-with-epstein), [The Local Norway](https://www.thelocal.no/20260428/norway-police-cooperate-with-france-on-epstein-probe)
- **Apr 29, 2026**: The couple's adult son died in Oslo. Their lawyers asked the public and media to avoid speculation; the couple's children are not suspected of any crime. [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/son-of-epstein-associate-under-investigation-dies-by-suicide-11899731)
- **Sep 23, 2026**: Rød-Larsen apologized for the relationship in an NRK documentary. See [Terje Rød-Larsen](Terje_Rod_Larsen.md).

## Evidence Posture

- The charge is a formal suspicion under Norwegian law, not a conviction. No trial date has been reported.
- The facts above come from the Norwegian government, Norwegian and international press, and the DOJ release. This repo has not reviewed the underlying Økokrim case file.

## Why This Matters

- Juul, her husband Terje Rød-Larsen, and former PM Thorbjørn Jagland are the senior Norwegian figures charged in connection with Epstein.
- The Norway-France joint investigation team shows European prosecutors pooling evidence rather than waiting on U.S. legal-assistance requests (see [International Investigations](../topics/International_Investigations.md)).
