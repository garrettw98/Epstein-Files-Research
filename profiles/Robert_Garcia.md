## Summary

Robert Garcia leads Oversight Democrats in the Epstein investigation. In 2026 he pressed for Pam Bondi's testimony after her removal, organized the minority's Palm Beach field hearing with survivors, and demanded testimony from AG Todd Blanche and FBI Director Kash Patel.
