# Stacey Plaskett (U.S. Virgin Islands Delegate)

> **Status**: **DOCUMENTED CONTACT — NOT ACCUSED OF A CRIME**; survived a censure vote (Nov 2025); Democratic nominee for Virgin Islands governor (Aug 2026)
> **Last updated**: Sep 24, 2026

## Who She Is

Stacey Plaskett has been the U.S. Virgin Islands' nonvoting delegate to the House since 2015 and sits on the House Intelligence Committee. Epstein lived on Little St. James in her territory.

## The Documented Contact

- **Feb 27, 2019**: While Michael Cohen testified before House Oversight, Plaskett exchanged text messages with Epstein. The messages appear to show Epstein commenting on her questioning; after she finished, he texted "Good work." The texts were in estate records House Oversight released on Nov 12, 2025. [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/5613244-stacey-plaskett-epstein-ties/), [Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2025/11/18/plaskett-avoids-censure-over-epstein-texts/)
- **Her account**: Epstein was a constituent, it was not publicly known in February 2019 that he was under federal investigation, and she did not need his advice to question Cohen. She has described the messages as unsolicited input of the kind she routinely receives.

## Timeline

- **Nov 18, 2025**: The House rejected a censure resolution by Rep. Ralph Norman (H.Res. 888) that would also have removed her from the Intelligence Committee, **209-214**. Republicans Don Bacon, Lance Gooden, and David Joyce voted with Democrats. [ABC](https://abcnews.com/US/house-votes-censuring-plaskett-epstein-texts/story?id=127670527), [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2025/11/18/plaskett-epstein-censure/), [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-resolution/888/text)
- **2026**: Entered the race for governor of the Virgin Islands. [Lee Fang](https://www.leefang.com/p/epstein-linked-house-democrat-explores)
- **Aug 1-3, 2026**: Won the Democratic gubernatorial primary with about 48%, ahead of Lt. Gov. Tregenza Roach (32%) and former legislator Donna Frett-Gregory (20%). [Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2026/08/03/plaskett-moylan-advance-in-territorial-primary-elections/)

## Evidence Posture

- The texts are documented. What they show about Epstein's influence over her questioning is contested, and she denies any improper influence.
- She has not been accused of any crime, and no victim has made allegations against her.
