# Ted Waitt

> **Status**: **HOUSE OVERSIGHT WITNESS** (not accused of any crime) (Evidence Level: ● TRANSCRIPT)
> **Role in Repo**: Gateway, Inc. co-founder; Ghislaine Maxwell's romantic partner for several years in the early 2000s
> **Last updated**: Sep 23, 2026

## Summary

Ted Waitt, the billionaire co-founder of Gateway computers, was in a relationship with Ghislaine Maxwell for several years in the early 2000s. His name appears in DOJ releases under the Epstein Files Transparency Act, and he was one of seven people House Oversight asked for transcribed interviews on March 3, 2026. He appeared on April 30, 2026; the transcript was released May 13.

## Testimony (April 30, 2026)

- Said he interacted with Epstein **fewer than five times** and found him off-putting.
- Said he would never have spent years with Maxwell had he known what she was doing.
- Acknowledged paying Maxwell **$7.2 million** after their relationship ended.
- Said he regretted not looking into Epstein's 2008 guilty plea, and that Maxwell had downplayed its seriousness.
- Committee Democrats said they found it hard to believe he had no knowledge of his partner's conduct with Epstein.
