# Todd Blanche (U.S. Attorney General)

> **Status**: **GOVERNMENT OFFICIAL** — Attorney General (confirmed Aug 8, 2026); acting AG Apr 2 - Aug 2026; previously Deputy Attorney General
> **Role in Repo**: DOJ public response, redaction-process defense, named defendant in *Phang v. Blanche*
> **Last updated**: Sep 24, 2026

## Summary

Todd Blanche, President Trump's former personal lawyer, was the DOJ official most associated with public defense of the 2026 release operation while serving as Deputy Attorney General. After Trump removed Pam Bondi on April 2, 2026, Blanche became acting attorney general, was formally nominated in June, and was confirmed 50-49 on August 8. As AG he is the named defendant in *Phang v. Blanche*, the first lawsuit to produce court orders enforcing the Epstein Files Transparency Act, and faces a Sept. 24 court deadline under threat of possible contempt.

## What Is Documented vs Unresolved

### Documented
- Blanche issued no-new-prosecution messaging in February 2026.
- He has led DOJ since April 2, 2026, including during the Phang litigation, the IG and GAO reviews, and the foreign legal-assistance requests.
- A federal judge has found DOJ out of compliance with the Transparency Act in at least some respects under his leadership.

### Unresolved/Disputed
- Whether DOJ will comply with the Sept. 24 deadline or seek appellate relief.
- Whether DOJ will act on the Leon Black contempt referral and the Levine/Fekkai referral request.
- Whether the reported executor investigation signals a broader change from the February "no new prosecutions" position.
