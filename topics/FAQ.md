# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

> **Goal**: Simple answers to the most common questions.
> **Last updated**: Sep 23, 2026

## 1. Is there a "Client List"?
**No and Yes.** There is no single sheet of paper titled "Client List."
*   We have **Flight Logs** (passengers).
*   We have the **Black Book** (contacts).
*   We have **Court Documents** (mentions).
*   *Reality*: The internet often conflates these lists. Being in the "Book" does not mean someone was a "Client." AP's February 2026 review of internal FBI/DOJ material reported no evidence of a standalone client list. See [The No Client List Finding and Its Limits](The_No_Client_List_Finding_and_Its_Limits.md).

## 2. Why isn't Bill Clinton/Donald Trump in jail?
**Lack of Evidence.**
*   **Clinton**: Flew on the plane, but no victim has accused him of sexual acts in verified court documents. He and Hillary Clinton sat for House depositions in February 2026 and denied wrongdoing.
*   **Trump**: Accused in a 2016 lawsuit (withdrawn with no evidence); witness testimony in 2024 specifically said he was *not* seen on the island. In March 2026 DOJ posted FBI memos of 2019 interviews with a woman who alleged abuse by Epstein and Trump in the 1980s; those allegations are **unverified**, no charges followed, and the White House calls them baseless.
*   *Reality*: Flying on a plane with a bad man is not a crime. Proving they participated in abuse requires witness testimony or physical evidence, which (currently) does not exist for them.

## 3. Did Elon Musk go to the island?
**No evidence he did.**
*   Emails show Epstein *invited* him and that Musk discussed visiting in 2012 and 2013.
*   Musk says he declined; the emails show Epstein canceled one planned visit.
*   There are **no flight logs, photos, or witness testimonies** placing him on the island.

## 4. Is Ghislaine Maxwell talking?
**No.**
*   She is serving a 20-year sentence at Federal Prison Camp Bryan in Texas.
*   She invoked the Fifth Amendment before House Oversight in February 2026.
*   Her own petition to vacate her conviction was denied on Aug 25, 2026 as "demonstrably meritless."
*   Her lawyer says she will eventually ask for a pardon; the Senate unanimously opposed any clemency on Jul 29, 2026.

## 5. Who inherited Epstein's money?
**The "1953 Trust."**
*   The beneficiaries of this trust are **secret**.
*   It is managed by his lawyer and accountant, Darren Indyke and Richard Kahn. On Sep 23, 2026 the WSJ reported that federal prosecutors are criminally investigating them; they deny wrongdoing and have not been charged.
*   A large portion (~$125-150M) went to the **Epstein Victims' Compensation Program** to pay survivors, and the estate agreed in 2026 to pay up to $35M more to settle a class action. The rest remains in the trust.

## 6. Was Epstein a Spy?
**Probable but Unproven.**
*   The connections to **Robert Maxwell** (confirmed spy) and **Carbyne** (Intel tech) are strong.
*   **Alexander Acosta's** comment ("He belongs to intelligence") is the strongest hint.
*   In July 2026, House Judiciary Democrats opened a narrower inquiry into whether Epstein acted as an **unregistered foreign agent** for Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Russia, and Israel. See [FARA Inquiry](The_FARA_Foreign_Agent_Inquiry.md).
*   *Verdict*: It is the most logical explanation for his wealth and protection, but no agency has claimed him.

## 7. Have all the Epstein files been released?
**No.**
*   DOJ released about 3.5 million of roughly 6 million pages it identified (Data Sets 1-12, plus roughly 50,000 previously removed files reported restored in March 2026).
*   A federal judge found DOJ out of compliance with the Transparency Act in some respects and set a **Sept. 24, 2026** deadline. See [Phang v. Blanche](The_Phang_v_Blanche_Litigation.md).
*   The DOJ Inspector General and the GAO are both reviewing how DOJ redacted and withheld records.
*   A second law, the [Epstein Files Transparency Act II](The_Epstein_Files_Transparency_Act_II.md), has enough signatures to force a House vote after the midterms.

## 8. Is Pam Bondi still Attorney General?
**No.** President Trump removed her on Apr 2, 2026. Todd Blanche became acting AG and was confirmed 50-49 on Aug 8, 2026.

## 9. Has anyone new been charged?
**Not in the United States, as of Sep 23, 2026.**
*   **Reported investigations**: SDNY into executors Indyke and Kahn; New Mexico's reopened Zorro Ranch probe.
*   **Congressional referrals**: House Republicans asked DOJ to investigate allegations against Philip Levine and Frédéric Fekkai (both deny them); the House held Leon Black in contempt.
*   **Abroad**: Norway charged former PM Thorbjørn Jagland and diplomat Mona Juul with aggravated corruption; UK police arrested Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor and Peter Mandelson, both released under investigation and uncharged. France's probe has 26 alleged victims.

## 10. Why did the UK Prime Minister resign?
Keir Starmer announced his resignation on Jun 22, 2026 after months of pressure over his appointment of Peter Mandelson as ambassador to Washington, including revelations that Mandelson had failed security vetting. See [The UK Reckoning (2026)](The_UK_Reckoning_2026.md).

## 11. Is the "suicide note" real?
**Unknown.** A note Epstein's cellmate says he found in July 2019 was unsealed on May 6, 2026, but neither the court nor DOJ has authenticated it. See [The Purported Suicide Note](The_Purported_Suicide_Note.md).
