# 📚 Further Reading & Resources

> **Goal**: Verified sources for deeper research.

## 📖 Books
*   **"Nobody's Girl"** by Virginia Giuffre (2025).
    *   *The posthumous memoir of the primary whistleblower.*
*   **"Perversion of Justice"** by Julie K. Brown.
    *   *The definitive account of how the Miami Herald exposed the deal.*
*   **"Relentless Pursuit"** by Bradley Edwards.
    *   *The inside story from the lawyer who fought Epstein for 12 years.*
*   **"Epstein: Dead Men Tell No Tales"** by Dylan Howard.
    *   *Covers the intelligence angles and Ben-Menashe claims.*
*   **"One Nation Under Blackmail"** by Whitney Webb.
    *   *Deep dive into the organized crime/intelligence fusion history.*

## 📺 Documentaries
*   **"Jeffrey Epstein: Filthy Rich"** (Netflix).
    *   *Good overview, features survivor testimony.*
*   **"House of Maxwell"** (BBC).
    *   *Excellent deep dive into Robert Maxwell and the family history.*
*   **"Epstein's Shadow: Ghislaine Maxwell"** (Peacock).
    *   *Focuses on her role.*
