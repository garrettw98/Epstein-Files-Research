> **Status**: **VERIFIED PHILANTHROPY / STRATEGIC DONATIONS**

Epstein didn't just hide on his island; he bought the goodwill of the local community to ensure his privacy.

## 1. The "Good Neighbor" Strategy
*   **Schools**: Epstein donated **100+ Microsoft Surface tablets** and computers to Virgin Islands public schools.
*   **The Science Fair**: He funded the Department of Education's science fair, paying for travel and accommodation for winners.
*   **The Antilles School**: His company, Southern Trust, donated thousands to the "Annual Fund" of this exclusive private school on St. Thomas, where the local elite sent their children.

## 2. Political Contributions

    *   *Significance*: This gave him a direct line to the Governor's mansion.

## 3. The "Summer Camp"
*   **The Program**: He funded a summer camp for children with intellectual disabilities.
*   **The Purpose**: These acts of charity made it socially difficult for local officials to investigate the "generous billionaire" next door.

## Verdict
Epstein used strategic philanthropy to embed himself in the fabric of St. Thomas society, creating a "human shield" of local support that lasted until his 2019 arrest.
