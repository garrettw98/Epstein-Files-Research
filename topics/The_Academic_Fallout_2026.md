# The Academic Fallout (2026)

> **Status**: **ONGOING** — several reviews still open (Harvard, Yale); House Judiciary Democrats' university inquiry continuing
> **Last updated**: Sep 24, 2026

## Summary

The January 30, 2026 DOJ release put correspondence between Epstein and dozens of academics into public view. Universities responded with leaves, internal reviews, and resignations, and House Judiciary Committee Democrats opened an inquiry into whether Epstein used university ties to recruit and control victims. This page tracks **institutional actions**. Being named in the files, or corresponding with Epstein, is not evidence of a crime. Where a review cleared someone, that is recorded.

## The House Judiciary Democrats' University Inquiry

- **Jan 14, 2026**: Ranking Member **Jamie Raskin** wrote to Columbia (acting president Claire Shipman) and NYU (president Linda Mills) after survivors told committee Democrats that Epstein lured young women by promising help with admission and tuition at those schools. He asked for records back to 1990 involving Epstein, Maxwell, Darren Indyke, and Richard Kahn. [Columbia letter](https://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/democrats-judiciary.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/2026-01-14-raskin-to-shipman-columbia-re-epstein_0.pdf), [NYU letter](https://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/democrats-judiciary.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/2026-01-14-raskin-to-mills-nyu-re-epstein.pdf), [Press release](https://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/epstein-survivors-reveal-to-judiciary-democrats-that-epstein-used-relationships-with-new-york-university-columbia-university-to-lure-and-silence-victims-by-promising-them-admission-tuition)
- **Mar 28, 2026**: The committee granted Columbia an extension to produce records. [Columbia Spectator](https://www.columbiaspectator.com/news/2026/03/28/house-judiciary-committee-grants-columbias-extension-request-to-hand-over-epstein-related-correspondence-university-official-says/)
- **Jun 17, 2026**: Raskin expanded the inquiry to **Harvard** (president Alan Garber) and **Bard** (board chair James Cox Chambers), with records due Jul 1. He called Bard's WilmerHale review "too narrowly tailored," asked about emails suggesting Epstein coordinated with Botstein to place two foreign-born women (later identified as abuse victims) at Bard's former Russian partner, Smolny College, and asked Bard to make Botstein available for a transcribed interview. Harvard said it was continuing the review it began in November 2025. [Press release](https://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/judiciary-democrats-expand-investigation-into-how-epstein-leveraged-ties-to-universities-to-further-his-crimes-and-lure-victims), [Bard letter](https://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/democrats-judiciary.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/2026-06-17-raskin-to-chambers-bard-re-epstein.pdf), [Garber letter](https://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/democrats-judiciary.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/2026-06-17-raskin-to-garber-harvard-re-epstein.pdf), [Harvard Crimson](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2026/6/17/raskin-slams-harvard-epstein-probes/)
- As a minority inquiry, it has no subpoena power. No public findings have been issued.

## Interpretation Notes

- **Cleared vs. departed:** Ohio State's review cleared Landon of involvement in crimes, and Columbia said Axel broke no policy. The Bard review found no illegality but faulted judgment. Departures here reflect institutional judgments about conduct and reputation, not findings of criminal wrongdoing.
- **Admissions allegations:** Survivors' accounts that Epstein used promises of university admission as a recruitment tool are **allegations** made to congressional Democrats; the Columbia dental-school case is the one documented instance of an irregular admission tied to Epstein.
- **Open reviews (as of Sep 24):** Harvard (Nowak), Yale (Gelernter).
