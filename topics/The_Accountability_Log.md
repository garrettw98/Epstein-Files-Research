# 🧾 The Accountability Log: Who Apologized?

> **Status**: **PUBLIC RECORD TRACKER**

After the 2019 arrest, everyone in Epstein's orbit had to make a choice: Apologize, Deny, or Hide. Here is the scorecard.

## 🔴 The Deniers (Total Denial of Wrongdoing)
*   **Prince Andrew**: Settled for millions but admitted no liability. Maintained he "never met" the victim.
*   **Bill Clinton**: Denies knowing about the crimes. Spokesperson said he hadn't spoken to Epstein in a decade. Denies visiting the island.
*   **Donald Trump**: Claimed he fell out with Epstein years ago ("not a fan"). Denies visiting the island.
*   **Alan Dershowitz**: Vehemently denies all allegations. Sued his accuser (settled/dropped).

## 🟡 The "Regretters" (Admitted Contact, Denied Knowledge)
*   **Bill Gates**: Called his meetings a "huge mistake" and an "error in judgment" but maintained they were solely for philanthropy.
*   **Reid Hoffman**: Apologized for visiting the island, stating, *"It was a mistake to trust him."*
*   **Jes Staley**: Expressed "regret" for his relationship with Epstein but denied knowing about the crimes. (Regulators disagreed, banning him).
*   **Les Wexner**: Claimed he was "deceived" and "betrayed" by Epstein. Apologized to the Wexner Foundation community for the association.
*   **Tom Pritzker**: Called the association "terrible judgment" and retired as Hyatt executive chairman effective immediately on Feb 16, 2026. *(Corrected Sep 23, 2026; earlier text said "by end of 2026.")*
*   **Crown Princess (now Queen) Mette-Marit**: Apologized in February 2026 and told NRK in March that Epstein "manipulated and deceived" her.
*   **Ehud Barak**: Apologized publicly on Feb 13, 2026 and said he regretted ever meeting Epstein.
*   **Kathryn Ruemmler**: Told House Oversight (Jul 15, 2026) that Epstein was "a masterful liar" who used her and others to legitimize himself.
*   **Bill Gates**: Told House Oversight (Jun 10, 2026) he never knew of Epstein's crimes and that Epstein "contemplated blackmailing" him.
*   **Keir Starmer**: Said in April 2026 he should not have appointed Peter Mandelson; resigned as prime minister in June.
*   **Terje Rød-Larsen**: "I deeply regret today the relationship I had with Epstein" (NRK documentary, Sep 23, 2026).

## 🟢 The "Cooperators" (Forced or Voluntary)
*   **Darren Indyke & Richard Kahn**: The estate lawyers. They settled with the USVI and agreed to cooperate with authorities to avoid liability. **Sep 23, 2026:** the WSJ reported they are now the subjects of an SDNY criminal investigation; they deny wrongdoing and have not been charged. See [Estate Executors Investigation](The_Estate_Executors_Investigation.md).
*   **JPMorgan Chase**: "Regretted" the association in court filings (after paying $290M).
*   **Deutsche Bank**: Acknowledged "error" in taking him on as a client (after paying $75M).

## 🟣 The Refusers (2026)
*   **Leon Black**: Walked out of his House Oversight interview (Jun 26, 2026) after declining NDA questions, skipped a subpoenaed deposition, sued the committee, and was held in contempt of Congress (Sep 16, 2026).
*   **Ghislaine Maxwell**: Invoked the Fifth Amendment before House Oversight (Feb 9, 2026) while seeking clemency; her post-conviction petition was denied Aug 25, 2026.
*   **Pam Bondi**: Declined to appear for her subpoenaed deposition after leaving office; later gave a transcribed interview (May 29, 2026) in which she declined many questions.
