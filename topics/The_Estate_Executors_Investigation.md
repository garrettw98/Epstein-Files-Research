# The Estate Executors Investigation: Darren Indyke and Richard Kahn

> **Status**: **REPORTED CRIMINAL INVESTIGATION; NO CHARGES** (Evidence Level: ◐ MAJOR REPORTING)
> **Subjects**: Darren K. Indyke (Epstein's longtime lawyer) and Richard D. Kahn (Epstein's longtime accountant), co-executors of the Estate of Jeffrey E. Epstein
> **Last updated**: Sep 23, 2026

## Summary

On **September 23, 2026**, the Wall Street Journal reported that federal prosecutors in the Southern District of New York have opened a **criminal investigation** into Indyke and Kahn. According to the reporting, prosecutors have spent recent months scheduling interviews with potential witnesses and requesting documents about their dealings. The Journal said it could not determine which charges prosecutors are considering. **Both men deny wrongdoing, and neither has been charged.** Opening an investigation does not mean charges will follow.

If confirmed, this is the first reported federal criminal investigation of anyone in Epstein's circle since Ghislaine Maxwell's 2020 arrest.

Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/jeffrey-epstein-estate-executors-investigation.html), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/alisondurkee/2026/09/23/doj-investigating-executors-of-epsteins-estate-report-says/)

## Background

- Indyke and Kahn worked for Epstein for more than a decade until his death in August 2019 and witnessed the **"1953 Trust"** that Epstein signed two days before he died. See [The 1953 Trust Beneficiary Blind Spot](The_1953_Trust_Beneficiary_Blind_Spot.md).
- As co-executors they have controlled the estate, reported at more than $100 million in remaining assets, and its records since 2019.
- **Dec 17, 2025**: Senate Democrats led by Ron Wyden asked DOJ and the FBI why the two had never been questioned as part of the federal Epstein investigation. [Senate Finance](https://www.finance.senate.gov/ranking-members-news/with-epstein-file-deadline-approaching-wyden-leads-democrats-demanding-to-know-why-doj-has-never-questioned-epsteins-core-trafficking-henchmen)
- **Mar 11, 2026**: House Oversight deposed Kahn about Epstein's finances. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/jeffrey-epstein-accountant-richard-kahn-408bd7342060174bd456eb08bdd13488)
- **Mar 11, 2026**: BBC reported that two longtime aides continued to control parts of Epstein's money and records after his death. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cdjm7rxjxneo)
