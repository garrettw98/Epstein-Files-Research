# The Foreign-Agent (FARA) Inquiry

> **Status**: **CONGRESSIONAL MINORITY INQUIRY OPEN** (Evidence Level: ◐ DOCUMENT-BASED ALLEGATIONS)
> **Opened by**: Rep. Jamie Raskin (D-MD), Ranking Member, House Judiciary Committee
> **Letter date**: July 20, 2026 (announced July 21)
> **Addressees**: Acting AG Todd Blanche (DOJ), Secretary of State Marco Rubio, and Jay Clayton
> **Last updated**: Sep 23, 2026

## The Allegation

House Judiciary Democrats say newly surfaced records in the Epstein files show Epstein cultivated relationships with foreign governments, including **Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Russia, and Israel**, while apparently trying to influence Trump administration policy toward them, without registering under the **Foreign Agents Registration Act (FARA)**.

According to the letter, Epstein:
- acted to advance the interests of the governments of Saudi Arabia and Kuwait;
- advised Russia's government on how to shape its dealings with President Trump;
- worked as an ongoing consultant for former Israeli Prime Minister **Ehud Barak**;
- used his long friendship with Trump to pursue business from foreign governments and actors after the 2016 election.

**These are the committee minority's characterizations of documents, not findings by a court or DOJ.** Failure to register under FARA is a federal crime, but Epstein died in 2019 and cannot be prosecuted. The inquiry's practical targets are DOJ's and State's knowledge and any unexamined enforcement questions involving others.

## What Was Requested

- All DOJ and State records on Epstein's contacts with foreign governments.
- Identification of any FARA investigations involving Epstein.
- An explanation of why no enforcement action was pursued.
