# The LA28/Wasserman Fallout

> **Status**: **RELEASE-PHASE FALLOUT TRACKER**

## Summary

This topic tracks the 2026 fallout around LA28 organizing chair Casey Wasserman after reporting on Maxwell-linked emails and subsequent political pressure related to Olympic governance.

## Timeline

- **Feb 14, 2026**: AP reported Wasserman would sell his talent agency after email-related disclosures.
- **Feb 18, 2026 (reported)**: Additional reporting said local officials called for him to step down as LA28 chair.

## Why This Matters

- LA28 is a major global event organization; leadership controversy carries public-governance and reputational implications.
- The case is a model for how file-release disclosures affect institutions not directly part of criminal proceedings.

## 2026 Update (Mar 17 - Sep 23)

- **February**: LA28's board reviewed Wasserman's contacts with Epstein and Maxwell and backed him; Mayor Karen Bass and other officials called for his resignation; he said he would not resign. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/olympics/story/_/id/48969633/casey-wasserman-says-resign-chair-2028-olympics)
- **Jul 28**: His former agency, renamed The Team, announced a Providence Equity-backed buyout of his remaining stake (Providence already owned about 60%; Sportico reported the deal at about $3.4 billion), with Michael Watts as the next CEO. [Sportico](https://www.sportico.com/business/finance/2026/providence-equity-wasserman-agency-sale-billion-1234940141/)
- **Status (Sep 23)**: Wasserman remains LA28 chair.
