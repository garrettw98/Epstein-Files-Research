# The Massie Floor Speech (Aug 31, 2026)

> **Status**: **CONGRESSIONAL ALLEGATION — NOT A CHARGING DOCUMENT**
> **Speaker**: Rep. Thomas Massie (R-KY)
> **Last updated**: Sep 24, 2026

## What Happened

On **Aug 31, 2026**, Rep. Thomas Massie read 14 names on the House floor, calling them Epstein "co-conspirators" who should be investigated and, where the evidence supports it, prosecuted. He used the same speech to launch the discharge petition for the [Epstein Files Transparency Act II](The_Epstein_Files_Transparency_Act_II.md), which reached 218 signatures on Sep 18. [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/6062468-massie-names-epstein-coconspirators/), [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/thomas-massie-names-14-people-he-says-should-be-investigated-over-epstein-12389523), [Political Wire](https://politicalwire.com/2026/09/01/massie-names-epstein-co-conspirators/)

This followed the Feb 10, 2026 floor reading by Massie and Rep. Ro Khanna of six names DOJ had redacted. See [Ro Khanna](../profiles/Ro_Khanna.md).

## How to Read This List

- **This is one member of Congress's allegation.** It is not an indictment, a DOJ finding, or a court ruling. The Constitution's Speech or Debate Clause shields floor remarks from civil and criminal liability, so the speech itself cannot be tested in a defamation suit.
- **Everyone named who has commented publicly denies wrongdoing.** Two of the 14 are dead.
- **Reasons for inclusion differ.** Some names come from FBI co-conspirator designations, some from civil allegations, some from correspondence in the DOJ release. The table records what this repository has documented, not Massie's reasoning, which he did not lay out name by name.

## Aftermath

- **Sep 1**: Seven women sued Fekkai in New York state court, citing Epstein's use of his salon; Forbes linked the filing to Massie's list. [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/alisondurkee/2026/09/02/epstein-victims-sue-top-hairdresser-after-rep-massie-names-alleged-co-conspirators/)
