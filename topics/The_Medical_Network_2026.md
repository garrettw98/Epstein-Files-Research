# The Medical Network (2026)

> **Status**: **UNDER REVIEW** — hospital committee, state health department review, and clinic departures; no disciplinary findings announced
> **Last updated**: Sep 24, 2026

## Summary

The 2026 DOJ release included emails, lab results, and billing records showing how Epstein obtained medical care for himself and for young women around him. It showed house calls, expedited appointments, and a concierge plan that covered Epstein and several women. The records led to an internal review at Mount Sinai, a physician's departure from two concierge practices, and a New York State Department of Health review. **None of these has produced a public finding of misconduct.** Physicians named in the documents have said they treated adult patients and did not know of any illegal conduct.
