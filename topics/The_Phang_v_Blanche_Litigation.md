# Phang v. Blanche: Court Enforcement of the Epstein Files Transparency Act

> **Status**: **ACTIVE** (Evidence Level: ● COURT RECORD)
> **Case**: *Phang v. Blanche*, No. 1:26-cv-01417 (D.D.C.)
> **Judge**: U.S. District Judge Emmet G. Sullivan
> **Plaintiff**: Katie Phang, attorney and independent journalist
> **Defendant**: Todd Blanche, in his official capacity (acting AG from Apr 2, 2026; confirmed AG Aug 8, 2026)
> **Last updated**: Sep 24, 2026

## Why This Case Matters

The Epstein Files Transparency Act (Pub. L. 119-38) required DOJ to publish its Epstein and Maxwell records and to redact only narrow categories (chiefly victim-identifying information). Until April 2026 the only pressure on DOJ to comply was political. *Phang v. Blanche* is the first lawsuit to produce court orders enforcing the Act. The records it targets overlap with the gaps this repository has tracked since February, and they are also what state and foreign prosecutors say they need.

## What the Court Ordered DOJ to Address

Based on reporting on the June 25 order and later proceedings:

1. **Eight email exchanges** in which the sender or recipient is redacted, including correspondence about a **"torture video"** and sexual activity with young women, some reportedly involving minors. (In February, Rep. Massie and DOJ identified one "torture video" correspondent as Sultan Ahmed bin Sulayem; see [his profile](../profiles/Sultan_Ahmed_Bin_Sulayem.md).)
2. The **2007 draft federal indictment**, never filed, with the names of potential co-conspirators redacted. See [The Draft Indictment](The_Draft_Indictment.md).
3. A **2019 email** referencing alleged co-conspirators whose identities are obscured.
4. **FBI interview notes**, including handwritten notes of four 2019 interviews with "Jane Doe 4," a woman who alleged Epstein abused her from about 1983 and took her to meet Donald Trump, whom she also accused. **These allegations are unverified, the FBI never brought charges, and the White House calls them baseless.** Relatives told The New Republic she is "living off the grid" and fears retaliation. [TNR](https://newrepublic.com/post/212554/woman-accused-trump-abuse-teen-living-fear-retaliation)
5. **Non-English records** DOJ had declined to review for release.
6. A complete **redaction log** explaining every redaction or withholding.

## Interpretation Notes

- **What is established:** a federal judge has found DOJ out of compliance with the Act in at least some respects and has personally reviewed unredacted versions of 10 records.
- **What is not established:** what the unredacted records show. Until the court orders release or DOJ produces them, the identities behind the redactions remain unknown. Do not infer names from redaction context alone. See [The No Client List Finding and Its Limits](The_No_Client_List_Finding_and_Its_Limits.md).
- **Watch for:** what DOJ filed on Sep 24, whether Sullivan opens civil contempt proceedings, and how the D.C. Circuit rules on the stay motion in No. 26-5299.
