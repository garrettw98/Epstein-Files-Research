# 🏝️ The Properties: Where It Happened

Epstein didn't just operate in one place. He had a network of luxury properties that facilitated his crimes.

## 🏝️ Little St. James ("Pedophile Island")
*   **Location**: U.S. Virgin Islands.
*   **Acquired**: 1998 for $7.95 million.
*   **The Setup**: A 70-acre private compound.
*   **The Temple**: A bizarre, stripe-painted building with a gold dome. Theories ranged from a religious temple to a secure elevator shaft. (Blueprints reveal it was likely a gym/music room).
*   **Significance**: The primary location for his most secretive abuse. Victims described being trapped there with no way to escape.

## 🏙️ The Manhattan Mansion
*   **Location**: 9 East 71st Street, NYC.
*   **Size**: One of the largest private homes in Manhattan (50,000 sq ft).
*   **The Mystery**: Originally owned by **Les Wexner**. Epstein moved in and eventually the deed was transferred to him for $0 (or $1).
*   **Surveillance**: This is where the hardwired camera network in bedrooms was discovered.
*   **The "Door"**: Victims described a heavy wooden door at the front that was always locked, trapping them inside.

## 🌵 Zorro Ranch
*   **Location**: Stanley, New Mexico.
*   **Size**: 10,000 acres.
*   **The "Baby Breeding" Plan**: Epstein told scientists he wanted to use this ranch as a base for inseminating women with his DNA.
*   **The House**: A massive mansion (30,000 sq ft).
*   **Status**: Sold in 2021.

## 🌴 The Palm Beach House
*   **Location**: Florida.
*   **Significance**: The center of the 2005-2008 investigation.
*   **The Operation**: This is where he recruited local high school girls for "massages."
*   **Status**: Bought by a developer in 2021 and **demolished**.

## 🇫🇷 The Paris Apartment
*   **Location**: Avenue Foch, Paris.
*   **Significance**: Epstein was returning from here when he was arrested in 2019.
