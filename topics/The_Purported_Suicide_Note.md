# The Purported Suicide Note (Unsealed May 6, 2026)

> **Status**: **UNAUTHENTICATED** (Evidence Level: ◐ COURT-RELEASED DOCUMENT, AUTHENTICITY NOT ESTABLISHED)
> **Released by**: U.S. District Judge Kenneth M. Karas (S.D.N.Y.), in the criminal case of Nicholas Tartaglione
> **Requested by**: The New York Times (motion filed Apr 30, 2026)
> **Last updated**: Sep 23, 2026

## What Happened

- **July 2019**: After the July 23 incident in which Epstein was found with neck injuries, his then-cellmate **Nicholas Tartaglione** says he found a note. See [July 23 Incident](July_23_Incident.md).
- **Apr 30, 2026**: The New York Times asked the court to unseal the document, which had been filed in Tartaglione's case. [Spectrum News](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/us/snplus/politics/2026/05/01/nicholas-tartaglione-jeffrey-epstein-purported-suicide-note-nyt-unsealing-request-metropolitan-correctectional-center)
- **May 6, 2026**: Judge Karas released it. **The court did not vouch for its authenticity or assess its chain of custody**, treating those issues as irrelevant to the unsealing decision. [Reuters via US News](https://www.usnews.com/news/us/articles/2026-05-06/new-york-judge-releases-purported-epstein-suicide-note), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/07/nx-s1-5814808/jeffrey-epstein-suicide-note), [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/judge-releases-note-that-cellmate-says-he-found-after-epsteins-suspected-suicide-attempt)

- "It is a treat to be able to choose one's time to say goodbye."
- "Watcha want me to do — burst out cryin! No fun - not worth it."

See [TIME](https://time.com/article/2026/05/07/alleged-jeffrey-epstein-suicide-note-released-by-new-york-judge/) for a fuller account.

## How to Weigh It

| Consideration | Note |
| :--- | :--- |
| Provenance | Comes from Tartaglione, who was convicted of four murders and sentenced to four consecutive life terms in 2024, and who has petitioned the president for a pardon |
| Authentication | Neither DOJ nor the court has verified that Epstein wrote it |
| Timing | Allegedly found after the July 23 incident, about three weeks before Epstein's death on Aug 10, 2019 |
| Relevance to the death | Even if authentic, it would not by itself explain the events of Aug 9-10 (guard falsification, camera failures, unit staffing). See [The MCC Guard Case](The_MCC_Guard_Case.md) |

**Bottom line:** The note is a real court-released document of unverified authorship. It should not be cited as proof of Epstein's state of mind without authentication.
