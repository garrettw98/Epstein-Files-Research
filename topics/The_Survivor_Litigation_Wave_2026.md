# The Survivor Litigation Wave (2026)

> **Status**: **ACTIVE** — at least ten survivor-driven civil matters filed, settled, or decided in 2026
> **Last updated**: Sep 24, 2026

## Summary

In 2026 survivors moved from the estate compensation program and bank settlements to a broader set of targets: Epstein's associates, service providers, a second-tier bank, the federal government, and Google. Two things drove it. The DOJ release gave plaintiffs documents to cite, and New York City reopened expired abuse claims under its Gender-Motivated Violence Act. **Complaints are allegations.** Defendants' denials are recorded below, and none of these civil cases has produced a finding of liability against an individual.

## The Legal Opening: NYC's Gender-Motivated Violence Act Lookback Window

- The City Council passed Int. 1297-A 48-0 on Nov 25, 2025. Mayor Eric Adams vetoed it, and the Council **overrode the veto on Jan 29, 2026**.
- The law revives expired claims for gender-motivated violence that occurred on or before Jan 9, 2022. The window runs from **Jan 29, 2026 to about Jul 29, 2027**, and it lets survivors sue people and entities that enabled or concealed abuse.
- The amendment answered a 2025 ruling that had thrown out more than 450 lawsuits filed under the earlier window.
- Sources: [Sanford Heisler summary](https://sanfordheisler.com/blog/new-amendment-to-nycs-gender-motivated-violence-act-gmva-expands-survivors-rights-and-reopens-lookback-window-through-july-2027/), [amNY](https://www.amny.com/politics/woman-abused-by-epstein-repares-lawsuit-nyc/)

## Patterns

- **Repeat plaintiffs.** Several of the same women appear in the Wexner, *Christiansen v. Indyke*, and Fekkai complaints, so organized plaintiffs' counsel is working through a list of defendants under the NYC window.
- **Defendants are moving down the chain.** After JPMorgan ($290M, 2023), Deutsche Bank ($75M, 2023), and Bank of America ($72.5M, 2026), plaintiffs have turned to a smaller bank (FirstBank), service providers (Fekkai), and the government itself (FBI, DOJ).
- **Venue fights matter.** The Wexner case ended over whether a federal court had jurisdiction, not on the merits.
- **Congress is referencing the same people.** Fekkai was named in Sarah Kellen's House interview (May 21) and on Massie's Aug 31 list. See [The Massie Floor Speech](The_Massie_Floor_Speech_Aug_2026.md).
