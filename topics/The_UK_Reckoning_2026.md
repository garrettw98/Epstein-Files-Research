# The UK Reckoning (2026): Arrests, a Prime Minister's Resignation, and a Possible Public Inquiry

> **Status**: **ONGOING** (Evidence Level: ◐ OFFICIAL STATEMENTS + MAJOR REPORTING)
> **Figures**: Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor, Peter Mandelson, Keir Starmer, Andy Burnham, Sarah Ferguson
> **Last updated**: Sep 23, 2026

## Summary

No country outside the United States has seen larger institutional consequences from the Epstein files than the United Kingdom. In 2026, a former prince and a former cabinet minister and ambassador were arrested, and the prime minister who appointed that ambassador resigned. Neither man has been charged, and both investigations reportedly depend partly on U.S. records that DOJ has not yet provided.

## Interpretation Notes

- **Misconduct in public office** is a common-law offence requiring willful misconduct by a public officer that amounts to an abuse of public trust. See [The Conversation explainer](https://theconversation.com/what-exactly-is-misconduct-in-public-office-and-could-peter-mandelson-be-convicted-275122).
- Arrest in England and Wales is an investigative step, not a charge.
- The UK cases illustrate a broader pattern documented in [International Investigations](International_Investigations.md): foreign prosecutors now say access to unredacted U.S. records, the subject of [Phang v. Blanche](The_Phang_v_Blanche_Litigation.md), may determine whether cases proceed.
