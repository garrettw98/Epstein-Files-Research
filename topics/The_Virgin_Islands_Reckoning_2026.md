# The Virgin Islands Reckoning (2026)

> **Status**: **ONGOING — NO CHARGES; LIMITED LEGISLATIVE INQUIRY**
> **Last updated**: Sep 24, 2026

## Summary

Epstein lived and allegedly abused girls and women on Little St. James in the U.S. Virgin Islands for two decades, and he relied on the territory's tax programs and officials. The Jan 30, 2026 DOJ release and follow-up reporting brought that relationship back into focus: a sitting governor's 2019 texts offering Epstein help in a permit dispute, a fired attorney general's account to the FBI of pressure over Epstein, and a single senator's inquiry into how agencies handled him. **No USVI official has been charged.** Gov. Albert Bryan Jr. denies wrongdoing. Accounts below are attributed to the people who gave them.
